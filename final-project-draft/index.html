Welcome to Mars Explorer

NASA has spent decades exploring Mars using robotic spacecraft and rovers. These missions allow scientists to study another planet without sending humans to its surface.

Mars rovers travel across the planet, collect scientific information, and send photographs back to Earth. Their discoveries help scientists learn more about Mars and its history.

This website provides information about NASA's Mars Rover Program, including the different rovers, their missions, and the discoveries they have made.

Exploring the Martian Surface

How Mars Rovers Work

Mars rovers are robotic vehicles that travel across the planet's surface. They use cameras, sensors, and other scientific instruments to collect data.

Scientists on Earth use the information collected by the rovers to study Mars and plan future missions.

Important Mars Rover Missions

1997

Sojourner

Sojourner became the first rover to successfully explore the surface of Mars. It helped NASA test robotic exploration technology.

2004

Spirit and Opportunity

Spirit and Opportunity landed on Mars in 2004. Both rovers explored different locations and searched for evidence of ancient water.

2021

Perseverance

Perseverance landed on Mars in 2021. Its mission includes studying ancient environments and collecting rock samples for possible future return.

Why Is Mars Important?

Mars is one of the most studied planets in our solar system. Scientists believe that liquid water once existed on its surface, making it an important place to study the possibility of ancient life.

NASA continues to explore Mars to understand how the planet has changed over time and to prepare for future space exploration missions.
